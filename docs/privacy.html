Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

This website

paidextension.dev is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. It sets no cookies and runs no advertising or cross-site tracking. The only thing stored in your browser is your light/dark theme preference, in localStorage, if you toggle it.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages are read and how fast they load. Cloudflare adds a small script to each page that reports page views, referrers, country, browser and device type, and page-load timings. Cloudflare states it uses no cookies or localStorage for this and does not fingerprint visitors by IP address or user agent. GitHub and Cloudflare, as host and network, may log requests (IP address, user agent) under GitHub's privacy statement and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Purchases

Checkout happens on Polar, which acts as merchant of record. Polar collects your email, payment details and billing country, and — to deliver the kit — the GitHub account to grant repository access to. We receive your name, email, country and GitHub username so we can provide support and updates. Payment card data never reaches us. See Polar's privacy policy.

Email

If you write to hello@paidextension.dev we keep the correspondence for as long as needed to help you. We do not run a newsletter and do not share addresses with anyone.

The kit itself

Extensions you build with PaidExtension talk to your Cloudflare Worker, your Paddle account and your Resend account — never to us. The kit contains no telemetry, phone-home or license check back to House of Arqam Ventures LLC. It ships a template privacy policy describing what your extension actually does, for you to publish with your product.

Your rights

Email us to access, correct or delete the purchase information we hold about you. Refund requests are handled as described in the terms.
